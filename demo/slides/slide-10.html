Why we didn’t just use one risk number.

Adding defensive NULL-checks raises cyclomatic complexity. A blended score would punish the fix.

Shin & Williams (2010) found only a weak correlation (r ≈ 0.30) between cyclomatic complexity and real vulnerabilities — a poor proxy for safety, and a terrible one for measuring a safety fix.
